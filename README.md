## 🌟 What is ClassTrack?

**ClassTrack** is a modern, beautifully designed, and feature-rich academic management app built for students, university scholars, and lifelong learners. It replaces messy paper schedules and complex spreadsheets with a sleek, all-in-one digital hub for your daily classes, exams, attendance goals, vacation breaks, and academic progress.

## 🔥 Key Feature Highlights

### 🧩 1. Dynamic & Customizable App & Phone Launcher Widgets
- **1-Tap In-App Dashboard Personalization**: Tailor your Home dashboard screen by toggling widgets on/off and reordering them to suit your study workflow.
- **9 Rich Modular In-App Widgets Included**:
  1. **🌴 Active Holiday Alert Banner**: Real-time notification of vacation breaks and paused attendance alerts.
  2. **⏳ Happening Now / Next Class Card**: Live period status and real-time countdown timer to your next class.
  3. **🃏 1-Swipe Attendance Deck**: Interactive card deck for fast gesture-based attendance logging.
  4. **⚡ Quick Action Hub**: 1-tap QR share/import, weekly timetable, bunk calculator, phone launcher widget setup, and vacation setup.
  5. **📊 Attendance Health Gauge**: Overall percentage badge, attendance tally, and safe bunk margin indicator.
  6. **📅 Today's Class Timeline**: Chronological period schedule showing room numbers, teachers, and log status.
  7. **📈 Weekly & Monthly Analytics**: Graphical attendance breakdown and bunk risk trends.
  8. **🎯 Upcoming Exam Countdown**: Countdown card for nearest midterms and finals.
  9. **💡 Daily Productivity Tips**: Smart advice and attendance maintenance pro tips.
- **📱 Phone Launcher Home Screen Widgets (Android AppWidget & iOS WidgetKit)**:
  - Keep live class schedules, attendance health %, and exam countdowns directly on your phone's home screen launcher!
  - **6 Native Launcher Widget Styles**:
    1. **🗓️ Weekly Schedule Timetable Widget**: Full Mon–Sun overview showing class count per day and today's active highlight.
    2. **↔️ Day Schedule (Horizontal) Widget**: Side-by-side horizontal period chips with room numbers and class start times.
    3. **↕️ Day Schedule (Vertical) Widget**: Chronological top-to-bottom timeline with teacher names and lecture rooms.
    4. **⏳ Happening Now / Next Class Countdown Widget**: Live period status with real-time minute countdowns.
    5. **📊 Attendance Health & Safe Bunk Meter**: Real-time percentage gauge, attended/held count, and safe bunk margin.
    6. **🎯 Exam & Deadline Tracker**: Countdown badge for nearest exam or assignment deadline.
  - **Live Auto-Sync**: Automatically syncs launcher widgets in the background whenever class schedules, attendance, or exams are updated in ClassTrack.
  - **How to Add Launcher Widgets**:
    1. Tap **Phone Widgets 📱** on the Home screen or Settings screen.
    2. Tap **Sync Launcher Widgets Now 🔄**.
    3. Go to your phone's home screen launcher, long-press empty space, tap **Widgets**, search for **ClassTrack**, and drag your favorite widget onto your home screen!

### 📊 6. Attendance Analytics, Bunk Predictor & "What-If" Simulator
- **Projected Attendance Scenarios Table**:
  - Goes beyond basic bunk counts—displays a live scenario matrix showing resulting percentages for attending or missing future classes:
    - *Attend next 5*: `81.2%`
    - *Attend next 3*: `79.8%`
    - *Miss 1 (Tomorrow)*: `76.5%`
    - *Miss next 2*: `74.6%` *(Shortage Warning)*
- **Interactive "What Happens If I Miss Tomorrow?" Simulator**:
  - Select any subject or your overall timetable to instantly simulate resulting percentages before making a decision.
- **Safe Absence Warnings**:
  - Live alerts when safe absences drop low (e.g. `⚠️ You have only 1 safe absence remaining.` or `🚨 Critical: 0 safe absences left!`).
- **Smart Bunk & Recovery Calculator**:
  - *Need a break?* Calculates exactly how many upcoming lectures you can safely skip while staying above your target (e.g. 75%).
  - *Fallen behind?* Calculates how many consecutive classes you must attend to recover your percentage.
- **Weekly & Monthly Analytics Bar Charts**: Visual bar charts displaying attendance rates per weekday (Monday–Sunday) and 4-week trend evolution.
- **Bunk Trends & Streak Counter**: Automatically highlights your *Most Bunked Day* (e.g. Friday) and tracks your consecutive 100% attendance streak.
- **Tinder-Style Attendance Deck**: Mark daily attendance with intuitive physical gestures—Swipe Right for Present, Left for Absent, or Up for Cancelled.
- **Subject History & Retroactive Logging**: Tap any subject to view complete session logs and modify past attendance entries.
